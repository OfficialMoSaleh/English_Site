Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our lessons, schedules, tutors, and learning materials.

How does the free 25-minute trial session work?

Your free trial session connects you 1-on-1 with an accredited native tutor. They evaluate your spoken fluency, identify strengths, and build your personalized learning syllabus. No payment information is required.

Can I choose between American and British English accents?

Yes, absolutely. Our tutors represent diverse native accents from the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. You can select teachers based on your preferred accent.

Can I reschedule classes if my work schedule changes?

Yes, 100%. You can reschedule or cancel any session free of charge up to 4 hours prior to the lesson start time directly from your student dashboard.

Do I receive a certificate upon finishing my course level?

Yes. Upon completing any CEFR level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, or C2), you will receive a verifiable digital certificate to showcase on LinkedIn and your resume.
